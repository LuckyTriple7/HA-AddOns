# Schwimmen (31) — Rules

**Goal:** Collect cards of one suit with the highest value. The player with the lowest hand loses a life. The last player standing wins.

---

## Game Components
- French deck with 32 cards (7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace in ♥ ♦ ♠ ♣)
- 3 players
- Each player starts with 3 lives

## Card Values

| Card | Value |
|---|---|
| Ace | 11 |
| King, Queen, Jack | 10 |
| 10 | 10 |
| 9 | 9 |
| 8 | 8 |
| 7 | 7 |

## Calculating Hand Value

The hand value is the **sum of all cards of one suit**. Only the best suit counts.

**Examples:**
- ♥A + ♥K + ♠9 → Hearts: 11+10 = **21**, Spades: 9 → **Hand value: 21**
- ♦A + ♦10 + ♦K → Diamonds: 11+10+10 = **31** (Maximum!)
- ♠J + ♥J + ♦J → Three of a kind = **30.5 points**

**Special hands:**
- **31** (Ace + two face cards of one suit): Instant round win, only the weakest player loses 1 life
- **Fire** (3 Aces): Instant round win, **all** others lose 1 life
- **Three of a kind** (3 same ranks): Counts as 30.5 points

---

## Round Sequence

### 1. Dealing
Each player receives **3 cards**. Additionally, **3 cards are placed face-up** on the table.

### 2. Turns (clockwise)
Starting left of the dealer, each player has these options:

- **Swap 1 card:** Exchange a hand card for a table card
- **Swap all:** Exchange all 3 hand cards for all 3 table cards
- **Pass:** Do nothing (skip turn). You may pass as often as you like
- **Knock:** Announce last round — all other players get exactly **1 more turn** (swap or pass), then hands are compared

**When all players pass consecutively** (without anyone swapping in between): The 3 table cards are removed and replaced by **3 new cards** from the deck. If not enough cards remain, the round ends immediately.

### 3. Round End
The round ends when:
- A player **knocks** and all others have taken their final turn
- A player reaches **31** or **Fire** (immediate end)
- Not enough cards in the deck to replace after all players passed

### 4. Scoring
- With **31**: Round ends immediately, only the weakest player loses 1 life (normal scoring)
- With **Fire** (3 Aces): **All** others lose 1 life
- Otherwise: The player with the **lowest hand value** loses 1 life
- On **tie**: All players with the lowest value lose 1 life each

## Lives and Swimming

| Lives | Status |
|---|---|
| ♥♥♥ | 3 lives — all good |
| ♥♥♡ | 2 lives |
| ♥♡♡ | 1 life — be careful! |
| 🏊 | **Swimming** — last chance! |
| 💀 | Eliminated |

- Losing your last life puts you **swimming** — one last chance
- Losing while swimming means you're **out**
- The last remaining player **wins**

---

## Strategy Tips
- **Collect one suit** — focus on one suit with high cards
- **Ace + face card** in one suit is already strong (21+)
- **Knock at the right time** — when you have a good hand, pressure the others
- **Watch the table cards** — swap specifically for what improves your suit
- **Swap all** — only when the table cards are clearly better than your hand
- **Pass wisely** — if everyone passes consecutively, 3 new table cards appear
- **Play aggressively against swimmers** — knock early when opponents have no lives left
