## Yahtzee — Rules

Goal: collect as many points as possible over **13 rounds**. The highest total at the
end wins.

### A turn
- You roll **5 dice** and may roll **up to three times** per turn.
- After each roll you may **hold** any dice (tap them) and re-roll the rest.
- Then you record the result in **exactly one open box** of the score sheet. Each box
  can be used only **once** — if nothing fits you must "scratch" a box (0 points).

### Upper section
- **Ones–Sixes:** sum of the dice showing that number.
- **Bonus:** if the upper section reaches **at least 63 points**, you get **+35**.

### Lower section
- **Three / Four of a kind:** at least 3 resp. 4 equal → sum of **all** dice.
- **Full house** (3 + 2 equal): **25** points.
- **Small straight** (4 in a row): **30** points.
- **Large straight** (5 in a row): **40** points.
- **Yahtzee** (5 equal): **50** points.
- **Chance:** sum of all dice (always allowed).

### Yahtzee bonus
If you already have **50** in the Yahtzee box and roll **another Yahtzee**, you get a
**+100 bonus** — and may use it as a **joker** in a matching box.

### Opponents
You play against **1 or 2 AI opponents** (selectable difficulty). Everyone rolls by the
same rules; the sheet shows every player's score.
