# 20 AB — Rules

**Goal:** Get from 20 points down to exactly 0. The first player to reach 0 wins.

---

## Game Components
- French deck with 32 cards (7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace in ♥ ♦ ♠ ♣)
- 3 players

## Starting: Who Goes First?

At the start, each player draws a card. The **highest card** determines who goes first (= chooses trump and leads). After that, the starting player rotates clockwise.

## Round Sequence

### 1. Hearts Blind? (before looking at cards!)

Before the starting player sees their cards, they may declare **Hearts Blind**:
- **Hearts Blind:** Hearts is trump, all points count **×4**, everyone must play — without knowing your own cards!
- **No:** Normal round, continue with step 2

**Hearts Blind** is high-risk: 3 tricks = −12 points, but 0 tricks = +20 penalty! Often the last resort when you have many points and opponents are close to 0.

### 2. First Deal (2 cards)
Each player initially receives only **2 cards** (revealed now, unless Hearts Blind).

### 3. Choose Trump
The **starting player** chooses a trump suit or a special option:
- ♥ Hearts, ♦ Diamonds, ♠ Spades or ♣ Clubs
- 🎲 **Next card** — a card is drawn from the deck, its suit becomes trump. The card goes to the starting player.
- The choice is based on the 2 hand cards — a tactical risk!

**Clubs special rule:** When **Clubs** is chosen as trump, **everyone must play** — no opting out!

**Next card special rule:** The drawn card goes to the starting player's hand. If the drawn card is **Clubs**, everyone must play (like clubs trump). For any other suit there is **normal bidding** — so you may opt out.

### 4. Bidding — with only 2 cards!
The other players (clockwise after the starting player) decide "Play" or "Pass":
- The **starting player always plays** (they chose trump after all)
- Playing means you're **active** — your tricks count for scoring
- Passing means you're **inactive** — your score stays unchanged
- **At most 1 player may pass** — if the first passes, the second must play
- With **Clubs trump** (including when the "Next card" is a club), bidding is skipped — everyone is automatically active
- **No opting out twice in a row:** Whoever opts out (passes) in a round **must play the next round**
- **Points lock:** Whoever has **fewer than 6 points** **may no longer opt out** and must always play
- **Important:** You decide with only 2 cards — this makes bidding a gamble!

### 5. Second Deal (3 more cards)
After bidding, each active player receives **3 more cards**, giving everyone 5 cards in hand. Players who passed receive no cards.

### 6. Exchange Cards (optional)
Each active player may swap up to **3 cards** from their hand:
- Select cards to swap (they get marked)
- Selected cards are discarded, new ones come from the deck
- You may also choose not to swap
- Swapping happens clockwise, starting with the starting player

### 7. Playing Tricks (5 tricks)
- The **starting player** leads the first card
- **Must follow suit:** If you have the led suit, you must play it
- If you can't follow suit, you may play anything (including trump)
- **Trick rules:** Trump beats everything, otherwise the highest card of the led suit wins
- Rank order: Ace (highest) > King > Queen > Jack > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7
- Whoever wins a trick leads the next trick
- Only active players participate in tricks

---

## Scoring

Only **active** players (who said "Play" or were forced in with Clubs/Next card) are scored:

| Tricks | Point Change |
|---|---|
| 1 trick | −1 (one point less) |
| 2 tricks | −2 |
| 3 tricks | −3 |
| ... | ... |
| 0 tricks (penalty!) | **+5** (5 points added!) |

**Hearts double:** When Hearts is trump, ALL points are doubled (×2). So:
- 2 tricks = −4 instead of −2
- 0 tricks penalty = +10 instead of +5

**Hearts Blind:** When Hearts Blind was chosen, ALL points count **four times (×4)**. So:
- 2 tricks = −8 instead of −2
- 0 tricks penalty = +20 instead of +5

**Bounce rule:** If someone would drop below 0 (e.g. from 3 to −2), the score bounces back positive: −2 becomes +2. You have to hit exactly!

---

## Examples

You have 7 points, Hearts is trump (×2), you take 3 tricks:
→ 3 × 2 = 6 points deducted → 7 − 6 = **1 point** remaining

You have 3 points, no Hearts, you take 4 tricks:
→ 4 deducted → 3 − 4 = −1 → **Bounces to 1**

You have 5 points, no Hearts, you take 0 tricks:
→ +5 penalty → 5 + 5 = **10 points** (ouch!)

You have 16 points, Hearts Blind (×4), you take 4 tricks:
→ 4 × 4 = 16 deducted → 16 − 16 = **0 points — You win!**

You have 16 points, Hearts Blind (×4), you take 0 tricks:
→ 5 × 4 = +20 penalty → 16 + 20 = **36 points** (bitter!)

## Strategy Tips
- **Trump choice is power** — the starting player sets the suit and always plays
- **Choosing Clubs = risk for all** — forces all players to participate, good when you're strong
- **Next card = gamble** — good with a weak hand; only when a club is drawn must everyone play, otherwise there is normal bidding
- **Passing is safe** — your score stays unchanged. But beware: you must play the next round, and below 6 points passing is fully locked
- **Hearts trump is double risky** — double reward but also double penalty
- **Hearts Blind is the nuclear option** — quadruple points without seeing your cards. Only choose when desperate or when opponents are close to winning!
- **Plan precisely:** If you're at 3 points, you need exactly 3 tricks (or 1½ with Hearts) — too many tricks bounce you back
- **Use exchanges:** Swap weak non-trump cards for better chances
