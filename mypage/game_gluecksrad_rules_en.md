# Wheel of Fortune — Rules

## Goal
Guess the hidden word or phrase by guessing letters. The player with the most money after 3 rounds advances to the finale.

## Gameplay

### Spin
- Spin the wheel. It lands on a money amount or a special field.
- **Money amount:** Choose a consonant. If it appears in the puzzle, you earn the amount **per occurrence**. You may continue playing.
- **BANKRUPT:** You lose all your round money. Next player.
- **LOSE A TURN:** You miss your turn. Next player.
- **RISK:** 50/50 — your round money is doubled or reset to zero.

### Buy a Vowel
- Costs **250** from your round balance.
- Choose a vowel (A, E, I, O, U). It is revealed but earns no extra money.
- You may continue playing afterward.

### Solve
- You may attempt to solve the puzzle at any time.
- **Correct:** You win the round and keep your round money.
- **Wrong:** Next player's turn.

### Special Characters
- **Ä, Ö, Ü** are automatically revealed with their base letter (A also reveals Ä).
- **ß** is revealed with S.
- The board displays the actual special characters.

## Rounds
- **3 rounds** are played, each with a new puzzle.
- After each round: the winner adds their round money to their total.
- Losers keep their existing total; round money is forfeited.

## Finale
- The player with the highest total plays the finale.
- **R, S, T, L, N** and **E** are automatically revealed.
- Choose **3 additional consonants** and **1 additional vowel**.
- **15 seconds** to solve the puzzle.
- **Correct:** Your total money is doubled!
