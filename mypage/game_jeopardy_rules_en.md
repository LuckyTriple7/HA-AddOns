# Jeopardy – Rules

A **quiz duel against the AI** on a classic Jeopardy board.

## The board

- **6 categories** with **5 tiles** each, from **200 to 1000** points.
- The point value hints at the difficulty: 200/400 = easy, 600 = medium,
  800/1000 = hard.
- Whoever has **control** (gold frame) picks the next tile. You start.

## A clue

1. You pick a tile – the question appears.
2. **Buzzer race:** hit the buzzer before time runs out. The AI has a randomized
   reaction time – if you are faster (and the AI isn't faster anyway), **you**
   get to answer. If you let it pass, the AI may take it if it "knows" the answer.
3. **Answering:** choose one of the four options.
   - **Correct** → value added, you keep control.
   - **Wrong** → value subtracted. If you buzzed first, the AI gets a second
     chance (and vice versa).
4. Nobody correct? No points, control stays.

## Daily Double ⭐

One hidden tile is the **Daily Double**. Only whoever reveals it plays it – with
no buzzer race. First place a **wager** (5 up to at least 1000 or your score).
Correct = win the wager, wrong = lose it.

## Final Jeopardy 🏁

When the board is empty, the **final clue** appears. You see the category and
**secretly** place a wager (0 up to your score). Then you and the AI answer the
same question. Wagers are settled.

## Winning

After Final Jeopardy, whoever has **more points** wins. Equal scores: a tie.

## Difficulty

It controls the AI: on **Easy** it buzzes and answers correctly less often; on
**Hard** it is fast and strong.
