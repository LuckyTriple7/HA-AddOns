# Mau Mau — Rules

**Goal:** Be the first to get rid of all your cards! The round winner earns a victory point. The first player to reach the required number of wins takes the match.

---

## Game Components
- French deck with 32 cards (7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace in ♥ ♦ ♠ ♣)
- 3 players
- Each player starts with 5 cards

## Gameplay

### 1. Dealing
Each player receives **5 cards**. One card is placed face-up on the **discard pile**. The remaining cards form the **draw pile**.

### 2. Turns (in order)
On your turn, you have two options:

- **Play a card:** Place a hand card on the discard pile that matches either the **suit** (♥♦♠♣) or the **rank** (7, 8, 9, …) of the top card
- **Draw a card:** If you have no matching card (or choose not to play), draw one card from the draw pile. If the drawn card matches, you may play it immediately

### 3. Draw pile empty?
When the draw pile runs out, all cards from the discard pile (except the top card) are shuffled and become the new draw pile.

---

## Special Cards

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **7** | The next player must **draw 2 cards** (and cannot play) — OR play another 7 (then the player after must draw 4, etc.) |
| **8** | The next player is **skipped** |
| **Jack** | Can **always** be played (regardless of suit/rank on top). The player **wishes a suit** that the next player must follow |
| **Ace** | The play direction is **reversed** (clockwise ↔ counter-clockwise) |

---

## Mau & Mau Mau

- A player with only **1 card** left calls **"Mau!"**
- A player who plays their **last card** calls **"Mau Mau!"** and wins the round

---

## Scoring

After a round, the **remaining cards** of the losers are counted:

| Card | Points |
|---|---|
| Ace | 11 |
| Jack | 20 |
| Queen, King | 10 |
| 10 | 10 |
| 9 | 9 |
| 8 | 8 |
| 7 | 7 |

The round winner earns **1 victory point**. The first player to reach the set number of wins takes the match.

---

## Strategy Tips
- **Save Jacks** — they are wildcards, especially valuable in the endgame
- **Stack 7s** — counter an opponent's 7 with your own 7 and pass the penalty along
- **Switch suits wisely** — after playing a Jack, wish for the suit you have the most of
- **Use 8s tactically** — skip the player who is close to winning
- **Play Aces strategically** — reversing the direction can shift the game in your favor
- **Count cards** — keep track of which cards have already been played
