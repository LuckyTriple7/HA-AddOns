# President — Rules

**Goal:** Be the first to get rid of all your cards and earn the rank of **President**! The first player to reach the target number of wins as President wins the match.

---

## Components
- French deck with 32 cards (7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace in ♥ ♦ ♠ ♣)
- 3 players (1 human, 2 AI)
- Each player gets **10 cards**, 2 go to the talon (unused)

## Ranks

After each round, players receive their rank:

| Place | Rank | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1st finished | **President** 👑 | Best of the round — earns 1 win point |
| 2nd finished | **Citizen** 👤 | Middle ground |
| 3rd (remaining) | **Beggar** 💀 | Last — must give cards next round |

---

## Gameplay

### 1. Dealing
Each player receives 10 cards. In **Round 1**, the player holding the **Club 7** (♣7) starts. From Round 2, the Beggar starts.

### 2. Card Swap (from Round 2)
Before play begins, a card swap takes place:
- The **Beggar** automatically gives their **2 highest cards** to the President
- The **President** chooses **2 cards** from their hand and gives them back to the Beggar
- Then the Beggar starts the new round

### 3. Playing Tricks
On your turn, play **1 to 4 cards of the same rank** (e.g., two Queens or three Nines):

- **Opening:** The first player plays any number of cards of one rank
- **Outbidding:** The next player must play the **same number of cards** of a **higher rank** — or pass
- **Passing:** If you pass, you're out for the current trick
- **Trick won:** When all others have passed, the last player wins the trick. They clear the cards and open the next trick

### 4. Card Ranking
Normal: **7 < 8 < 9 < 10 < Jack < Queen < King < Ace**

During Revolution (see below): **Ace < King < Queen < Jack < 10 < 9 < 8 < 7**

### 5. Finishing
A player with no cards left is **finished** and no longer participates in tricks. Play continues until only one player has cards — they automatically become the Beggar.

---

## Special Rule: Revolution

When a player plays **4 cards of the same rank** at once, a **Revolution** is triggered:

- The **card ranking** is **reversed** (7 becomes the strongest card, Ace the weakest)
- Another Revolution cancels the reversal
- The Revolution stays in effect for the rest of the game (even across rounds)

---

## Scoring

- The **President** of each round earns **1 win point**
- The first player to reach the target number of wins (3, 5, or 7) wins the match

---

## Strategy Tips
- **Play low cards first** — save your high cards for the endgame
- **Hold pairs and triples** — multiple cards at once are harder to beat
- **Pass strategically** — sometimes it's better to lose a trick to open the next one
- **Count cards** — remember which high cards have already been played
- **Use card swaps wisely** — as President, you can strategically get rid of weak cards
- **Watch for Revolution** — if you have many low cards, a Revolution could win you the game
